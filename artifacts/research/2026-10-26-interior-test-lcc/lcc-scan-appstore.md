# LCC Scan

Photo & Video

Free · Designed for iPad. Not verified for macOS.
iPhone, iPad

LCC Scan is the companion app for XGRIDS spatial camera. Use your phone to connect and control the device, view the camera feed, adjust settings, and manage your 3D scan. We also support the generation of 3D models through mobile phone scanning, offering more diverse gameplay options.
