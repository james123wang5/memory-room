# LCC Scan

The Spatial Camera in Your Phone.

LCC Scan captures people, objects, and small scenes in 3D, right from your phone¹. The 3D scanning app that runs entirely on\-device, with no cloud upload, turning every shot into a detailed 3D model you can move around inside. Any angle you want, whenever you want it.
Watch the video

[Download for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.XGrids.lccscan.i18n)

## One minute to capture. Two minutes to your interactive 3D.²

Follow the on\-screen guide and walk in a circle around your subject. Go from object to complete 3D model with no special equipment or 3D experience needed.

Three capture modes

### Portrait Mode

Built for people and outfits. Keeps faces, clothing, and full poses clear from the front, the side, and the back.

### Object Mode

For figures, collectibles, and handmade pieces. Follow the guided path around the object so its shape, materials, and structure come through.

### Free Mode

No category limits. Capture whatever you want, however you want, and save the moment in 3D.

## FAQs

### Is LCC Scan Free to Use?

Yes, LCC Scan is free to download on the App store and Android App Stores, so you can start scanning people and objects from your phone at no upfront cost.

### Which Phones Support LCC Scan?

LCC Scan is available on iPhone 13 and later models running iOS 17 or later, as well as iPad Air 5 and later models. On\-device reconstruction on Android phones requires Android 15 or later.

### How to 3D Scan with Your Phone?

Open LCC Scan, choose a capture mode, then follow the on\-screen guide and walk around your subject. One minute to scan, about two minutes to your interactive 3D in Fast mode. No special equipment and no 3D experience needed.

### What Can I 3D Scan With My Phone?

People, outfits, pets \(still or sleeping\), figures, collectibles, handmade pieces, and small scenes. For buildings, campuses, and full sites, PortalCam, Lixel K2, and Lixel L2 Pro take over.
