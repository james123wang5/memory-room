Hi, thanks for this great work\!

I’m wondering if LiteReality is currently limited to reconstructing objects detected by Apple RoomPlan. For objects such as computers, lamps, blackboards, or monitors that RoomPlan does not detect, could we use other models \(e.g., Grounding DINO \+ SAM2\) to detect them and estimate their 3D positions/bounding boxes from RGB\-D data, and then use TRELLIS.2 to reconstruct the corresponding 3D assets?

Would such an approach be compatible with the current LiteReality pipeline?

Thanks\!
