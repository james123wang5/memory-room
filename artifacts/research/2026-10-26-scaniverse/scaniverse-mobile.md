# Get started with Scaniverse


Find guides for creating Sites, uploading scans from mobile or 360 cameras, and generating VPS maps, meshes, and Gaussian splats for reliable localization.


Before you begin, make sure you download Scaniverse from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scaniverse-3d-scanner/id1541433223) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nianticlabs.scaniverse) to your mobile device.
