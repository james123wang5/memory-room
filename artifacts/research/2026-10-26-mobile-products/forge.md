# Train splats on your own computer.

Forge pairs with Splatcatcher on your iPhone. Send it a capture, and it trains a Gaussian splat on your Mac or PC and sends the result back to your phone.
[Download for Mac ](https://github.com/warpgatelabs/splatcatcher-forge-releases/releases/latest)

Also available [for Windows](https://github.com/warpgatelabs/splatcatcher-forge-releases/releases/latest)
![Forge's Jobs window: the Headset training with Spirula Studio, and the Orchid done at 2.9M splats](https://splatcatcher.warp-gate.com/img/forge-jobs.webp) ![Splatcatcher's Train sheet on iPhone, listing the trainers on a paired Mac Studio and Gaming PC](https://splatcatcher.warp-gate.com/img/phone-train.webp) 
Captures go straight from your iPhone to your computer, at home or away. They never pass through our servers. 

## Three steps to your first splat.

Once per computer. After that, sending a capture is one tap.
1.  On your computer 1 

### Install Forge

**Mac:** open the disk image and drag Forge to Applications. It runs from the menu bar.

**Windows:** run the installer. Forge starts when you sign in and lives in the system tray.
2.  2 

### Add a trainer

Forge drives a Gaussian Splatting trainer on your computer. In Forge’s **Trainers** page, download **Spirula Studio** with one click.

On Windows with an NVIDIA GPU you can also use **LichtFeld Studio**: unpack it into Downloads or Desktop and Forge finds it.
3.  On both 3 

### Pair your iPhone

In Forge, open **Phones** and choose **Pair a phone**. In Splatcatcher, tap **Your PCs → Add a PC** and scan the code.

Then open any capture and choose **Send to PC**.

## What you need.

Your iPhone and computer should be on the same network to pair.

### Mac
- macOS 14 Sonoma or later 
- Apple silicon \(M1 or later\) to train with Spirula Studio

### Windows
- 64\-bit Windows 10 \(version 1809\) or Windows 11 
- A GPU with Vulkan for Spirula Studio, or an NVIDIA GPU for LichtFeld Studio

**“Windows protected your PC”?** The installer isn’t code\-signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
