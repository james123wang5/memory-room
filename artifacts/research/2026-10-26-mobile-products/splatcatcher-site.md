# Gaussian splats from your iPhone.

Splatcatcher scans with your iPhone's LiDAR, solves the camera poses on the phone, and exports a COLMAP dataset that's ready to train. Open it in your own trainer, or send it to Forge on your Mac or PC and view the finished splat on your phone.

## Know your scan worked before you leave.

Images, camera poses, and a colored point cloud are recorded together on the phone, so you can check coverage and fix the gaps while you're still there.

### See what still needs another angle.

The point cloud builds from LiDAR depth as you walk. Turn on Coverage and every surface shows how many angles have seen it, so you know where to go back.
- **1 angle 
- **2 angles 
- **3 or more, in true color
- Live warnings for motion blur, weak features, and tracking drift 
- Loop\-closure prompts that tell you when to revisit an area 
- 4K frames on supported devices, with blur rejection and shutter\-speed control

### Poses solved on the phone.

LiDAR records every camera pose as you scan. One tap refines them against the depth, corrects autofocus drift, and drops frames that don't fit. There's no structure\-from\-motion step on your computer.
**0.60px** Mean reprojection error on the Headset after one tap, down from 1.86 px
![The Headset's refined point cloud with a yellow frustum for each camera pose](https://splatcatcher.warp-gate.com/img/phone-poses.webp) 

### Your splat, back in your pocket.

Send a capture to Forge on your Mac or PC, at home or away. It trains the splat and sends it back to your iPhone, where you can orbit, pan, and zoom around it.

## From scan to splat.

The slow, uncertain part happens on site, where you can still fix it. Training happens wherever you like.
1.  On the iPhone 1 

### Scan

Walk around the subject while LiDAR builds the point cloud.
2.  2 

### Check

Turn on Coverage, find the surfaces seen from one angle, and rescan them while you're still there.
3.  3 

### Refine

One tap solves every camera pose on the phone.
4.  4 

### Export

AirDrop the COLMAP folder, open it in Files, or copy it over USB.
5.  On a computer 5 

### Train

Open it in your trainer, or tap Send to PC and [Forge](https://splatcatcher.warp-gate.com/forge) trains it and returns the splat to your phone.

## Opens in your trainer.

Every project is a standard COLMAP folder: images, per\-frame intrinsics and poses, and the colored LiDAR point cloud to initialize the splats.
