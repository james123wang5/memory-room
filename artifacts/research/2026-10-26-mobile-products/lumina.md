# Lumina 3D

Utilities

Free · In‑App Purchases
iPhone, iPad

Lumina 3D is a professional iPhone app for capturing production\-ready datasets for Gaussian Splatting workflows. Designed for creators, developers, and production pipelines, Lumina 3D enables high\-quality data capture for tools like Lightfield Studio, PostShot and Brush \(BETA\). Capture with full control Take full control over your dataset quality with manual camera settings: • Lock exposure, ISO, shutter, and white balance \(Kelvin\) • Ensure consistent lighting and color across all images • Avoid automatic camera adjustments during capture Efficient scanning workflow • Capture multiple scans and combine them into a single dataset • Continue scanning in multiple passes for complex scenes • Seamlessly build large environments step by step LiDAR \+ image capture • Record depth and RGB data simultaneously • Adjustable depth range \(0.15m – 10m\) • Filter unwanted foreground or background geometry Scan as you move • Automatic capture while moving \(“scan & go”\) • Efficient dataset creation without manual triggering • Optimized for smooth and fast workflows Export ready for production • Export datasets optimized for: • Lightfield Studio • PostShot • Brush • Designed for modern Gaussian Splatting pipelines Built for professionals Lumina 3D is designed for reliable, repeatable capture workflows and production\-ready results.
