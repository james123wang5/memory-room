# Scan the world in 3D.
All on your iPhone.

Walk around a room or an object while Scantic films and builds a 3D scan you can step inside. Everything is computed on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
[![Download Scantic on the App Store](https://scantic.app/assets/app-store-badge.svg) ](https://apps.apple.com/app/scantic/id6795293934)

Free on the App Store. iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

Built in the EU · Servers in Germany · End\-to\-end encrypted sharing

### Capture anything

Point your iPhone at a room, a corner or a single object and move around it in a slow circle. Scantic guides you while it films, so every side gets covered.

### Computed on the phone

The 3D scan is built right on your iPhone in a couple of minutes. No cloud, no account, no waiting on a server, and it works offline.

### Explore and share

Step inside the result and look at it from angles you never filmed. Your scans live in your library, ready to revisit and share whenever you want.

## Private by design. Built in the EU.

Scans are captured, computed and stored entirely on your iPhone. No account, no tracking, no uploads. Nothing leaves the device unless you share it, and even sharing is built so that we learn as little as technically possible.

### An EU product

Made in Vienna, Austria. Shared scans are stored on a server in Nuremberg, Germany, under EU law and the GDPR.

### End\-to\-end encrypted

Private and password protected links are encrypted on your iPhone before upload. The key travels inside the link itself and never reaches our server.

### No data collection

No account, no analytics, no tracking of any kind. Nothing is uploaded unless you share a scan yourself, and private links stay unreadable even to us.

[How sharing is encrypted](https://scantic.app/encryption/) [Read the privacy policy](https://scantic.app/privacy/) 

## How it works

Three steps from your camera to a scene you can walk around.
Step 1 

### Scan

Start a new scan and move in a slow circle around your subject. More light helps, and Scantic tells you when to slow down.
Step 2 

### Process

Your iPhone turns the capture into a 3D scene, entirely on the device. Pick between a faster result and finest detail.
Step 3 

### Explore

Walk through the finished scan in the built\-in viewer. The capture stays in your library, so you can process it again anytime.
