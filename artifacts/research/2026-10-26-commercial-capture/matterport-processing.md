How long does a model take to process?

Last updated Apr 13, 2026

Model processing times depend on several factors:
- **Upload speed. **Time it takes to upload from your device to the cloud.
- **Processing queue. **Certain times of the day see more models uploaded than others.
- **Model size. **This is biggest factor. Larger models take longer to process. A model with only [1 or 2 scans](https://support.matterport.com/s/article/What-is-processing?language=en_US) might finish in 30 minutes, while a model with 200\+ scans generally takes 24 to 48 hours. Models with difficult geometry also require more time to process.
- **Blur Faces. **If this option was selected when uploaded, this adds about 2 minutes per scan position. So if your model is 100 scan points, expect 3.3 hours to be added to the processing time.  

Most models process within eight hours.
- 24 hour processing is unusual.
- Longer than that is rare, but possible.

You will receive an email when processing is complete. This email is sent to the account that was logged in when the model was uploaded.

![](https://support.matterport.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Vw000000HEkv&feoid=00N5d00000JWIaQ&refid=0EMKd000001w8ZV)

You **cannot stop a model from processing** once it is uploaded. However, if a model has been processing for more than 24 hours, you can delete it by selecting the three dots next to the name and picking Delete. 

![](https://support.matterport.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Vw000000HEkv&feoid=00N5d00000JWIaQ&refid=0EMVw00000C6KGT)  

When you're logged in to your Matterport account, if you access the Showcase URL for a space that's still processing, you'll see a message saying, "This space is processing. Please check back later to view your model." This message appears only to users who are logged in and are collaborators on the space.

![processing.jpg](https://support.matterport.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Vw000000HEkv&feoid=00N5d00000JWIaQ&refid=0EM5d0000099oVF)

If you have a Classic plan, refer to the [What is processing? \(Classic Cloud Plans\)](https://support.matterport.com/s/article/What-is-processing-Classic-Cloud-Plans) article for billing considerations.
